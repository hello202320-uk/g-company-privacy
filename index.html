G Company Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how G Company handles information when users access the application and its online multiplayer services.

Information We Process

G Company may process limited information required to provide authentication and multiplayer functionality, including:

  • Meta Horizon User ID
  • Meta/Oculus username where made available through Meta Platform services
  • Authentication and session identifiers
  • IP address and basic technical/network information
  • Multiplayer identifiers required to connect users to game sessions

How We Use Information

This information is used to:

  • Identify and authenticate signed-in users
  • Associate users with their in-game account
  • Provide multiplayer functionality
  • Maintain game sessions
  • Operate and secure the service

Service Providers

G Company uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate its online services. These may include PythonAnywhere for backend hosting and Photon (Exit Games) for multiplayer networking and authentication infrastructure. These providers may process information necessary to provide their services.

Data Sharing

G Company does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the application, provide multiplayer functionality, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide requested functionality, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request deletion of information associated with their account or make other privacy-related requests by contacting the operator of G Company.

Children and Age Requirements

G Company is intended for users who meet the age requirements applicable to the application and Meta Horizon services. Information is handled in accordance with applicable platform requirements and law.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features, data practices, or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page.
